Praia de Iracema — map first, signs second

The Praia de Iracema waterfront in Fortaleza mixes public facilities, landmarks and old signs. Before drawing a single sign, a team of five needed to know what was there and where.

CourseSignage Design · 2026
TeamFive students, Unifor
My part3D survey map, delivery pipeline
DeliveredLocation map, 4 sign types, A3 drawings, talk

A tool for the team

I built an interactive 3D map of the waterfront, shared with the team by link, where everyone marked what the field survey found: existing signs, signs to remove, facilities, and the pieces we were proposing.

The survey became the delivered location map, and each sign's position became its justification.

Three icons from the shore — the sail, the sea and the sunset — give the system its language. Four pieces: urban totem, directional totem, indicative sign and interpretive sign.

Built to the standard

Front, side and top views with every dimension, materials and finishes chosen for salt air and sun, mounting height and structure. Accessibility follows NBR 9050: braille and relief, QR codes with audio, 7.1:1 contrast and a matte finish against glare.

The boards, the slides and the parts list were all generated from one data table, so changing a piece changed every document at once, and they never disagreed.
