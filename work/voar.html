Why does an NGO lose the donors who aren't companies?

Associação Voar runs education, culture and sport programmes for children and families in Fortaleza. Whole projects depended on a single corporate donor, while individual donors had almost no relationship with the organisation. We designed a donation app around that gap, tested it, and wrote it up as a paper.

CourseDesign Research · 2025.2
TeamGroup project, Unifor
MethodDouble Diamond, user-centred design
OutputApp prototype + published paper

Process

  1. Discover. A field visit to Voar's headquarters, plus desk research on giving in Brazil — the share of Brazilians donating money fell from 66% in 2015 to 52%.
  2. Focus the questions. A CSD matrix (certainties, suppositions, doubts) turned into a semi-structured interview script.
  3. Interview. Three interviews with Voar staff to fill the gaps the matrix exposed.
  4. Define. An affinity diagram connected the pains into user requirements.
  5. Benchmark. ShareTheMeal, the UN World Food Programme's app, as the reference for a low-friction donation flow.
  6. Develop. Four task flows — sign up and donate, follow activities and volunteer, shop, profile — then a low-fidelity prototype.
  7. Test. Usability tests with potential users; the feedback went into a mid-fidelity digital prototype.

What we found

Voar lost a percentage of every donation to a third-party platform, had no income of its own, and no ongoing relationship with recurring individual donors. The requirements that came out: a simpler sign-up before giving, a donation channel Voar controls, and continuous communication with the people who give.

Published

From project to paper

Plataforma digital de captação de recursos para a Associação Voar: uma ferramenta de otimização da conexão entre associação e doador. Proceedings of the VI Encontro de Curricularização da Extensão, Universidade de Fortaleza, 2026. ISSN 2965-9396.

An expanded version is being adapted for IHC 2026, the Brazilian Symposium on Human Factors in Computing Systems (Innovative Ideas and Emerging Results track).
