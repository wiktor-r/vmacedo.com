Visual identity

Roberto Kennedy — framing the light

A brand for a photographer who shoots hotels, architecture and companies. The briefing asked for minimalism, precision and professionalism; the answer is a monogram where two columns frame a point of light.

CourseVisual Identity Systems · 2026
TeamVictor + Leticia Frota Grangeiro
ClientRoberto Kennedy, photographer
DeliveredBrand, versions, applications, video, talk

Concept

From the client briefing came four anchors: hotel and corporate work, seriousness, structures — much of his work is façades, columns and interiors — and framing, which for him is essential.

Six sketch directions later, we chose an R and a mirrored K between two stems. The stems are the columns he photographs and the frame that crops a scene; the star in the gap is the point of light; the high-contrast serif carries the elegance hotels expect.

Six versions — primary, secondary, simplified, greyscale, line and negative — plus clear space, minimum sizes, an achromatic palette and type (Cormorant with Jost). We defended it in class in six and a half minutes, with an interactive deck and the video above.
