RUPTURA

A magazine about decolonial music: “self-expression that goes beyond the norm of colonial music”. Concept, palette, type, grid, master pages and the full page plan, laid out in InDesign.

Flatland, E. A. Abbott

An A5 edition of 128 pages, eight signatures of sixteen. EB Garamond at 12/15.6 pt, 32 lines a page, widows and orphans controlled, the author's own eleven figures, and crop marks for the printer.
